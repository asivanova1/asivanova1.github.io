📚 Managing references

May 17, 2026

When writing scientific papers, courseworks and dissertations, it is important to format the bibliography correctly. Here are the main tools and rules:

Reference managers

  • Zotero — free and powerful tool, allows collecting, organizing and citing sources. Plugins for Word and Google Docs available.
  • Mendeley — from Elsevier, also free, good for collaboration.
  • EndNote — paid but very functional, the standard in many universities.

Citation styles

  • GOST (for Russia) — used in theses and scientific articles.
  • APA, MLA, Chicago — international styles.

BibTeX / BibLaTeX

When working with LaTeX, BibTeX is used — a source database. Example entry:

@book{knuth1984,
  author    = {Knuth, Donald E.},
  title     = {The TeXbook},
  year      = {1984},
  publisher = {Addison-Wesley}
}

GOST formatting rules

  • Author's last name and initials, book/article title, journal, year, volume, issue, pages.
  • For online sources — URL and access date.

Tip: use reference managers from the early stages — it will save hours of time at the end.
